   ## Ingredients

   - 250g all purpose flour
   - 1/2tsp salt
   - 2tsp ground cinnamon
   - 210g room temperature butter
   - 2 eggs
   - 220g powdered sugar
   - 1tsp vanilla extract
  
   ## Steps

   1. In a bowl, mix the flour, salt and cinnamon. 
   2. In a secund bowl, mix the butter (creamed) and sugar until it has a fluffy and light consistency.
   3. Beat the eggs and vanilla and slowly pour into the butter mixture.
   4. Stir in the dry ingredients. 
   5. Divide the mixture into four equal parts then roll into 5cm diameter logs. Wrap and freeze until firm.
   6. Cut the logs into slices and bake 10-15min at 190ºC.
