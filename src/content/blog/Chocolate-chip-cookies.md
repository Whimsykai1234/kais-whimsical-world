   ## Ingredients

   - 115g room temperature butter
   - 30g granulated sugar
   - 70g brown sugar
   - 1/2tsp baking soda
   - 1 egg
   - 175g all purpose flour
   - 1/2tsp vanilla extract
  - A pinch of salt
   - 175g chocolate chips
   - 50g chopped walnuts
  
   ## Steps

   1. In a bowl, mix the butter and two sugars until you’ve got a fluffy consistency. 
   2. In another bowl, mix the egg and vanilla and slowly pour into the butter mixture. 
   3. Sift over the flour, baking soda and salt and stir. 
   4. Add the chocolate chips and walnut and mix to combine.
   5. Place scoops of the dough and bake 10-15min at 180ºC until lightly colored. 
   6. Transfer to a rack to cool.
