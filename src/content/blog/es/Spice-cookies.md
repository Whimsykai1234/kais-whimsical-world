   ## Ingredientes

   ### Masa

   - 180g harina
   - 100g mantequilla pomada
   - 100g azúcar moreno
   - 1 huevo
   - 1 cucharada grande de miel
   - 2g gengibre en polvo
   - 1g canela en polvo
   - 1g clavo molido
  
   ## Pasos

   1. Pour all the ingredients into a bowl and mix.
   2. Knead the dough until you’ve formed a ball.
   3. Let the dough rest for about 45min in the fridge.
   4. Spread the dough with a roller. Keep in mind they have to be thin.
   5. Cut and bake in the over 15-20min at 175ºC.
