   ## Ingredientes

   - 225g mantequilla pomada
   - 240g azúcar blanco
   - 230g queso crema
   - 5g levadura química
   - 1 huevo
   - 525g harina
   - 14g esencia de vainilla
   - 2g esencia de almendra
  
   ## Pasos

   1. In a bowl, mix the butter and sugar until you’ve got a fluffy and light consistency. 
   2. Add the egg, vanilla extract and almond extract and stir.
   3. Sift over the flour, baking soda and stir. 
   4. Let the dough rest in the fridge for a couple of hours.
   5. Spread the dough with a roller, cut and bake 8-10min at 180ºC. 
   6. Transfer to a rack to cool.
