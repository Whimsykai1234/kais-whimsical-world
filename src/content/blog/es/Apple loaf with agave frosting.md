## Ingredientes

### Bizcocho

- 70g azúcar blanco
- 70g azúcar moreno
- 120ml aceite neutro (yo uso aceite de girasol)
- 2 huevos grandes
- 180g puré de manzana
- 1tsp esencia de vainilla
- 60ml leche
- 240g harina
- 9g levadura química
- 6g sal
- 5g canela en polvo
- 1g nuez moscada
- 1.5g clavo molido
- 1g gengibre en polvo

### Frosting

- 160g queso crema
- 40g mantqeuilla pomada
- 25g sirope de agave
- 1tsp esencia de vainilla
- 160g azúcar en polvo

## Pasos

### Puré de manzana
Peel three medium sized ripe apples, take off the core and cut them in small chunks. Place them inside of a small pot with two tablespoons of water to prevent the apples from sticking to the pot. Cook for about 10-15min at medium-low heat with the pot's lid on or until they're soft. Drain excess water and blend until smooth.

### Bizcocho

- Preheat oven at 190ºC
- Mix the flour, baking powder, baking soda, salt, cinnamon, nutmeg, cloves and ginger.
- In another bowl, mix the two sugar, oil, egg, apple puree, vanilla extract and milk until it has got a smooth consistency. 
- Add the dry ingredients to the wet ones and combine them without overmixing them.
- Pour into a loaf pan and bake for 5 minutes, then reduce the heat to 175 and bake for another 55-65 minutes. 
- For the frosting, mix all of the ingredient until smooth, let chill in the fridge while the loaf is baking and the spread once it has rested. 

## Recomendaciones

Using red apples for your puree will give the loaf a milder taste, so I would recommend using green ones. If bought at a supermarket, make sure the puree is just water and apples. 

For the frosting, I used mascarpone cheese, as it gives a milder taste. If you'd rather have a stronger, cheesy flavor, just use plain light cream cheese. 

In case you didn't like cream cheese frosting but you still want to add something on top, you can cut a big green appple, place them in a pan and cook with a drizzle of honey and 5g of unsalted butter to get a delicious caramelized apple topping.
