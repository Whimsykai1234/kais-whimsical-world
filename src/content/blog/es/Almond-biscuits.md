   ## Ingredients

   - 100g almendra molida
   - 300g harina
   - 170g mantequilla pomada
   - 1 huevo
   - 130g azúcar blanco

   ## Steps

   1. Pour all the ingredients into a bowl and mix
   2. Knead the dough until you’ve formed a consistent ball
   3. Let the dough rest for about 30min in the fridge
   4. Extend the cookie dough with a roller so that it’s around 0.5cm thick.
   5. Cut and bake in the oven 15-20min at 160ºC.
