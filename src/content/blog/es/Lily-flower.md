## About the pattern

Cala lily flowers are one of the best options if you want a bouquet formed by just one type of flower. Make 8 of them and you'll get a super cute nosegay :3.

This pattern is divided in 3 steps: the leaf, the lily's core and the lily's petal. You'll need a different color for each part. You'll have to crochet each part separately, and then put them all together. 

## Pattern
Lily's petal
- Round 1: 10sc in magic ring

- Round 2: ch2, 10dc increase

- Round 3: ch1, [3sc, sc inc] x 2, [sc, sc inc] x 2 [3sc, sc inc] x 2

- Round 4: ch1, [4sc, sc inc] x 2, [2sc, sc inc] x 2, [4sc, sc inc] x 2

- Round 5: ch1, 5sc, sc inc, [2sc, sc inc] x 2, [3sc, sc inc] x 2, [2sc, sc inc] x 2, 5sc, sc inc

- Round 6: ch2, 6hdc, hdc inc, [3hdc, hdc inc] x 2, [4hdc, hdc inc] x 2, [3hdc, hdc inc] x 2, 7hdc

- Round 7: ch2, 7hdc, 16dc inc, (tr, ch4, (3st in the ch4), tr), 16dc inc, 7hdc

Lily's heart
- Round 1: 6sc in magic ring

- Rounds 2-10: 6sc

Lily's leaf
- Round 1: ch28

- Round 2: 2sc, 2hdc, 20dc, hdc, sc, (3sc), sc, hdc, 20dc, 2hdc, 2sc

Insert thin wire to forme the leaf's structure. 

- Round 3: sc all the way round
