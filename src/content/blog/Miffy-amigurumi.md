## Pattern
### Miffy's ears

- Round 1: 6sc in magic ring
- Round 2: 6sc inc (12)
- Round 3: [sc, sc inc] x 6 (18)
- Round 4 to 10: 18sc
- Round 11: [4sc, sc decr] x 3 (15)
- Round 12: 15sc
- Round 13: [3sc, sc decr] x 3 (12)

Make this pattern two times, one for each ear.
