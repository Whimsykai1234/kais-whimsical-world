## Ingredients

### Dough

- 200g Greek yogurt
- 20g sugar
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
- 9g yeast
- 540g flour
- 185g whole milk
- 110g butter
- 2 eggs
- 1 tsp salt

### Filling

- 115g butter
- 240g brown sugar
- 12g cinnamon
- 1 tsp vanilla extract

### Frosting

- 250g cream cheese
- 60g butter
- 210g powdered sugar
- 1 tsp vanilla extract

## Steps

1. Activate the yeast with the milk and the sugar.
2. Mix all the dough ingredients except for the butter.
3. Pour in the activated yeast.
4. Add softened butter in chunks and mix.
5. Let the dough rise 1 hour at room temperature or 8 hours in the fridge.
6. Spread the dough into a rectangle.
7. Mix the filling ingredients and spread them on the rectangle.
8. Cut and make 12 rolls.
9. Put on a tray and let rise for another hour.
10. Bake in the oven at 180ºC for 35-40 min.
11. Mix the frosting ingredients and spread once the rolls have chilled a bit.
