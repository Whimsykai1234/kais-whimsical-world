## Ingredients

- 4 bananas: 3 for the dough and 1 on top
- 67g brown sugar
- 115g room temperature unsalted butter
- 2 eggs
- 2tsp vanilla extract
- 115g greek yogurt
- 165g all purpose flour
- 1tsp baking soda
- 1/2tsp cinnamon
- 170g dark chocolate chips
